Most brands are showing up on social media every day — but still seeing zero real growth. Consistent posting is just the beginning. Here are the 5 mistakes we see most often, and how to fix them.

## 1. Posting Without a Strategy

Throwing content at the wall and hoping something sticks is not a strategy. Every post should serve a purpose — awareness, engagement, conversion, or retention.

**Fix:** Define your content pillars. For most brands, 3–4 pillars work best. For example: *educational content, behind-the-scenes, social proof, and promotional.*

## 2. Ignoring the First Hour After Posting

The algorithm rewards early engagement. If you post and disappear, you're leaving reach on the table.

**Fix:** Stay active for 60 minutes after posting. Reply to every comment, engage with similar content, and respond to DMs. This signals to the algorithm that your content is worth distributing.

## 3. Inconsistent Visual Identity

Scrolling through a feed that looks different every week destroys brand recognition. People should be able to identify your content before they even see your name.

**Fix:** Build a simple brand kit — 2–3 colors, 1–2 fonts, a consistent editing style. Stick to it across every post.

## 4. Writing Captions for Yourself, Not Your Audience

Long, self-focused captions that don't deliver value in the first line get skipped instantly.

**Fix:** Lead with a hook that speaks directly to your audience's problem or desire. Make the first line impossible to ignore.

## 5. Not Tracking What's Working

Posting without reviewing analytics is like driving without a map. You don't know what's working, what's not, or where to go next.

**Fix:** Set a weekly 15-minute analytics review. Look at reach, engagement rate, and saves. Double down on what's performing.
